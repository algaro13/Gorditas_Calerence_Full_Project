## Requirements
### Requirement: The landing says the system is practical and easy to use
The public landing SHALL lead with the message that the system is practical and easy to use, and SHALL highlight its features. Every feature it names SHALL exist in the product. It SHALL NOT show testimonials, customer counts or other claims the business cannot back.

#### Scenario: A restaurant owner opens the landing
- **WHEN** the landing loads
- **THEN** the headline says the point of sale is practical and easy to use, and the features shown below are ones the product has

### Requirement: The landing works on a phone
On a phone the landing SHALL let a visitor start the trial and sign in, every control SHALL meet the 44 px touch minimum, no text SHALL be under 14 px, and the page SHALL not scroll sideways.

#### Scenario: Signing in from a phone
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the landing on a phone
- **THEN** a "Iniciar sesión" control is visible and reachable, as is the button to start the trial
